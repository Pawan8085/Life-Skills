### Q. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)
* Pay full attention to the speaker without interrupting. <br>
* Maintain eye contact and use positive body language to show interest. <br>
* Listen carefully to understand the message instead of thinking about the reply. <br>
* Ask relevant questions to clarify doubts and gain better understanding. <br>
* Avoid distractions such as mobile phones or side conversations while listening. <br>
* Repeat or summarize important points to confirm understanding.


### Q. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)
* Focus on understanding the message rather than immediately giving advice or reacting.
* Ask questions if something is unclear instead of making assumptions.
* Show empathy and make the speaker feel heard and respected.
* Repeat or paraphrase the message in your own words to confirm understanding.
* Try to understand the speaker’s feelings and thoughts properly.
