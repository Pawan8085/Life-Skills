
# Learning Process

## Q. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a four-step learning method used to understand and master a topic by explaining it in simple words.

## Q. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Active (Focused) Mode of Thinking

In this mode, the brain concentrates deeply on a specific task or problem. It is used when we are actively studying, solving math problems, coding, or paying close attention to something. This mode helps us think carefully and logically.

### Diffuse Mode of Thinking

In this mode, the brain is relaxed and works in the background. It usually happens when we take a break, relax, walk, or sleep. This mode helps us connect ideas, think creatively, and find solutions from a different perspective.

## Q. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

* Deconstruct the skill into smaller parts.
* Learn enough to self-correct while practicing.
* Remove barriers and distractions from practice.
* Practice with focused effort for at least 20 hours.

## Q. What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

* Break complex topics into smaller and simpler parts before learning.
* Practice regularly instead of studying only at the last moment.
* Remove distractions like mobile phones and social media while studying.
* Focus on understanding concepts instead of only memorizing them.
* Take short breaks when stuck on a problem to refresh my mind.
