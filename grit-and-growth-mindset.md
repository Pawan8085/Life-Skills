Angela Duckworth explains that success is not only based on IQ or talent, but also on “grit,” which means passion and perseverance toward long-term goals. 


## Q. Paraphrase (summarize) the video (Growth Mindset Introduction:) in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
A fixed mindset is the belief that skills and intelligence are fixed and cannot change, so people often avoid challenges, fear mistakes, and take feedback personally. A growth mindset is the belief that abilities can improve through effort and learning, so people accept challenges, learn from mistakes, and use feedback to grow.



## Q. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
### What is the Internal Locus of Control?
Internal Locus of Control is the belief that success and failure mainly depend on a person’s own effort, actions, and decisions rather than luck or external factors. People with this mindset believe they can improve through hard work and persistence.

### What is the key point in the video?
The key point of the video is that people become more motivated and confident when they believe they have control over their own success. Encouraging effort, learning, and hard work helps build persistence and a positive mindset.
